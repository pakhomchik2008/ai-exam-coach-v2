# Phase 5 — PTE Academic

Same quality bar as GRE/GMAT: official structure, real scale, paper
language, verified curriculum. Pearson PTE Academic, 10–90 overall and
per skill. 22 item types after 7 Aug 2025 (Summarize Group Discussion,
Respond to a Situation). ~2 hours. Integrated scoring — one item can
feed several skills; we do not invent an average.

Speaking stays in the tree (unlike IELTS presets). The paper is a
headset at a computer; Teach/Drill still work without Pearson's scorer.

## Not this slice

- PTE Core, PTE Home, UKVI as a separate qualification
- Licensed Scored Practice Tests
- Live mic scoring against Pearson traits
